## 1. Complement vs normal truncation (p = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6)

**Command:**  
`run_complement_sweep(p_values=(2,3,4,5,6), gamma_values=(0.3, 1.0, np.pi, 2*np.pi))`  
(from `symmetry_reduction.orbit_symmetry`).  
Alternatively, `python -m symmetry_reduction.determinant_ratio_decay` includes complement truncation for p=5.

- **Truncation types:**  
  - **Complement:** keep α with |α^c| ≤ m (i.e. |α| ≥ n − m).  
  - **Normal:** keep α with |α| ≤ m.
- **Table columns:** **Truncation** — shown as ∣α^c∣≤m (compl m) or ∣α∣≤m (norm m). Then **n_kept**, **k99**, **r_s** (stable rank), **Energy%** (Frobenius energy captured).
- **n = 2p+1** (number of bits; max |α|).

- **kept (frac):** fraction of the *kept* (top k_99) subspace energy in block m.  
- **dropped (frac):** fraction of the *dropped* (remaining) subspace energy in block m.

## Definitions (for reference)

- **k_99:** number of singular directions needed to capture 99% of the Frobenius norm of H_log.
- **r_s (stable rank):** ‖H‖_F² / ‖H‖².
- **Block |α|:** Subset size m = 0..2p+1; block m is the set of indices α with popcount(α) = m.
- **Kept (frac):** Fraction of the *kept* (top k_99) subspace energy in each block.
- **Dropped (frac):** Fraction of the *dropped* (remaining) subspace energy in each block.
- **Complement truncation |α^c|≤m:** Keep rows/columns with |α^c| ≤ m. **Normal truncation |α|≤m:** Keep rows/columns with |α| ≤ m.
- **E_capt:** Frobenius energy captured by the orbit-reduced matrix (y=0).

To regenerate: run the commands in **symmetry_reduction** (see README_hessian_spectral.md and orbit_symmetry/README.md).
