Conclusion:
For the locally Krawczyk-certified stationary point at (beta, gamma) = (0.5434, -1.00), the discrepancy between the exact finite-size rate and the saddle action decreases over the tested range. The data are consistent with saddle-type finite-size corrections, but the present size range does not by itself prove that the discrepancy vanishes asymptotically or that this stationary point is the globally dominant contributing saddle.
